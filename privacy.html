Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Simmer (“we”, “the app”) is a recipe keeper for iPhone and iPad. We built it to keep your data yours.

Data you create

Your recipes, photos, cookbooks, meal plans and shopping lists are stored on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see this content and never receive it.

No account

Simmer does not require a sign-up and does not collect your name, email, or contacts.

On-device processing

Scanning recipes from photos happens entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. Your photos are not uploaded.

Web import

Importing a recipe from a website fetches that public page and reads its recipe data. No information about you is sent along.

Advertising

The free version of Simmer shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Subscribing to Simmer Premium removes ads.

Subscriptions

Simmer Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your payment details.

Children

Simmer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
